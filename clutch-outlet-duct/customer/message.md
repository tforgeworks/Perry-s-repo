Hi Tressa, it's Titan Forgeworks. We've finished the 3D model of your Polaris clutch outlet duct
(part 5439157), and pictures of it are attached.

The rectangular end, the mounting ear and most of the tube are copied exactly from the scan of
your part. The round outlet end, which had burned and torn off, we rebuilt to match the original.
We also cleaned up the scorched bend next to it.

Before we print it, could you check a few things for us? The outlet end is the part we had to
rebuild, so these make sure it fits your machine:

1. **Does the overall shape look right?** (pictures 1 to 3)
2. **Round end size.** Please measure the inside of the rubber boot that the round end of the duct
   pushes into. Ours is 66.8 mm (2 5/8 in) across the outside (A in picture 4). If the boot is
   missing or melted, measure across the outside of the round spout on the clutch cover instead.
3. **How far it goes in.** How deep does the round end push into the boot? Ours has 50 mm (about 2 in)
   of straight section (D in picture 5).
4. **Small tab on the rim.** The original has a small tab on the edge of the round end (C in pictures
   4 and 5). Is there a slot or notch for it in the boot? If there is, a photo of it would help us
   line the tab up.
5. **Your machine.** What year and model is your Sportsman? We want to double check the part
   number fits.
6. **Other heat damage.** Did anything near it get damaged too, like the boot, its clamp, the clutch
   cover outlet, or the push rivet that holds the mounting ear?

If you can, photos of the boot and the clutch cover outlet with a tape measure in the shot would
be perfect.

We'll print it in a heat-resistant material, because hot air comes out of the clutch. Once you
confirm, we'll get it printed.

Thanks!
